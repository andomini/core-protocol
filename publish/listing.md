## Description

A lone core sits at the centre of the grid, and the viruses are closing in.

Your core fires on its own. Your job is to make it stronger. Spend Energy
during the run on damage, attack speed, crits, range, health, regen, armor,
thorns and more. Every few waves, pick one of the protocols on offer: lightning chains,
freezing shots, overload bursts, firewalls and mining bonuses. Collect protocols of
the same kind to unlock powerful set bonuses and build a combo of your own.

Runs never end. Push as far as you can, then cash in Bits and Keys for
permanent upgrades in the Workshop, research in the Labs and card packs
with game-changing cards. Reach milestones to unlock harder tiers with
new rules and bigger rewards.

Features:
- Idle-style auto-fire with deep upgrade choices
- 25 protocols across 5 sets (Overload, Cryo, Chain, Mining, Firewall)
- 18 core stats, 25 lab research nodes and 20 collectible cards
- 6 tiers, each with its own twist
- Boss waves, ranged, fast and tank viruses
- Up to ×2 game speed, offline earnings
- Detailed run and lifetime stats
- Progress is saved automatically; an unfinished run can be continued

## How to play / controls

- The core shoots automatically at viruses in range.
- Tap upgrade buttons (ATK / DEF / UTIL tabs) to spend Energy during a run.
- When protocols are offered, tap one to take it. Matching protocols build up set bonuses.
- When the core falls, collect Bits and Keys. Spend them on Home: Workshop, Labs and Cards.
- Mouse (desktop) or touch (mobile). Pause and ×1/×2 speed at the top right.
